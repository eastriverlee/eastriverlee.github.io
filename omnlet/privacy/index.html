Privacy

Omnlet stores learning progress in an on-device database and keeps app preferences in the device's local preferences store. These records support spaced review, language and voice choices, favorites, settings, and resumable practice.

Omnlet has no account, analytics, or cloud sync service. The app does not send learning records or preferences to an Omnlet server.

Speech uses bundled on-device voices where available. Character playback uses a compatible voice installed on the device. System voice behavior follows the device operating system.

Your operating system may include Omnlet app data in device backups, depending on your backup settings.

When you export a backup, it can include progress, review history, favorites, helper languages, voice choices, learning and display settings, and saved template choices. You choose where to save or share it.

Problem reports include the sentence and meaning, app and operating system versions, and relevant answer or speech details. Omnlet only prepares a report when you open the report flow. You review it before choosing whether to share it. Any service you choose to share with handles the information under its own privacy terms.

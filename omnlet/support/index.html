Support

Practice and review

Practice words and generated sentences with spaced review. Build answers with word or character tiles, or use typed recall. Guided character composition appears for writing systems that define it.

Speech

Speech availability depends on the language and voices on your device. Bundled voices can speak offline in supported languages. Character playback requires a compatible installed system voice. Choose a voice from the language's voice settings.

Move progress to another device

Open Backup and restore to export your learning records. Transfer the backup yourself, then import it on the other device. Omnlet does not sync progress between devices.

Report a problem

Use the report control on a quiz or template screen. Choose a category, review the captured details, and add notes or a suggested answer. Share the report through your device's share options when you are ready. Reports may include the displayed sentence, answer details, app and operating system versions, and speech preparation details.

Contact

Email lee@eastriver.dev for help. Include your device, operating system, and Omnlet version, plus what happened and what you expected. You can attach a problem report prepared in the app.
